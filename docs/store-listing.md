DIGITIZE YOUR WARDROBE
Snap a photo of each piece. The background is removed automatically, and AI recognizes the type, color and details for you. Your whole wardrobe, organized and searchable, in your pocket.

OUTFITS MADE FOR YOU
Take a short style quiz to define your look. Sestina suggests outfits based on many factors, including the pieces you own and the weather. Save the ones you love and add them to your favorites.

## Ücretsiz deneme koşulları (mağazaya göre)

**App Store — TR:**

```
Ücretsiz deneme: Yıllık plan 7 günlük ücretsiz denemeyle başlar; aylık planda deneme yoktur. Deneme bitmeden en az 24 saat önce iptal etmezsen yıllık abonelik otomatik başlar ve yıllık ücret deneme bitiminde Apple Kimliği hesabından alınır. Aboneliğini App Store hesap ayarlarından istediğin zaman iptal edebilirsin; deneme içinde iptal edersen ücret alınmaz. Deneme her hesaba bir kez verilir.
```

**App Store — EN:**

```
Free trial: The yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial; the monthly plan has no trial. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the yearly subscription starts automatically and the yearly price is charged to your Apple ID account when the trial ends. You can cancel anytime in your App Store account settings; you won't be charged if you cancel during the trial. The trial is offered once per account.
```
